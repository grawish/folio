## LinkedIn

I built Folio so you can point at your resume and ask for a change.

Highlight a bullet and say “make this clearer.” Mark a section and ask for more space. Folio edits the LaTeX source and compiles an updated PDF on your Mac.

The 22-second video shows the workflow.

You can also chat beside the PDF, choose your AI model or use Auto, and compare or restore earlier versions. The .tex source is always there when you want to edit it yourself.

It’s available now as an early, unsigned preview for Apple silicon Macs. Connect Codex, Claude Code, or your own API account.

Try it: https://github.com/grawish/folio/releases/tag/v0.1.0-preview.4

If you use LaTeX for your resume, give it one edit you’d normally do by hand. I’d like to hear what worked and what felt awkward.

# Folio — brag plan

**What it is:** A chat-first LaTeX resume studio for Apple silicon Macs. Mark a spot on your PDF, tell your AI what to change, and it edits the TeX, rebuilds locally and checks the finished page.

**For:** Anyone rewriting a resume who wants LaTeX quality without fighting LaTeX.

**Sets it apart:** You point at the PDF instead of describing where something is. The agent verifies the rendered page. The source stays real, editable LaTeX compiled on your Mac — no TeX install.

**Angle:** "This part." — the whole product is the gap between pointing and a fixed page.
**Hook:** A cyan box drawing itself around a resume bullet, with the site's own line: *"This part." Now your AI can see it.*
**Highlights:** (1) Mark the PDF → attach to chat → send. (2) Folio's real four-step agent progress, then the page updates. (3) The Code tab: it's real LaTeX, compiled locally.
**Punchline:** folio. — Talk about your resume. See the result.
**Tone:** default — punchy, clean, soft transitions.
**Identity:** Folio dark theme tokens (`#101c2b` canvas, `#17283c` surface, `#76d4df` accent, `#83d6ae` green), Manrope + JetBrains Mono (bundled app fonts), lucide icons (same as app), real PDFs compiled by the bundled Tectonic and rendered with pdf.js (same as app).
**Share caption:** see share-copy.txt
